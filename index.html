Juju Digital is a small web agency founded in 2006 by Alex in close collaboration with Sue. We are a perfect synergy of developer and designer offering comprehensive skills and combined experience of over 45 years in branding, digital design, technical development and strategic web marketing.

Being small keeps us agile and responsive. Through our local and global network of associates we can, when the need arises, create larger virtual teams. Whatever the size of your project, you will get our personal attention and a package tailored to your needs. By avoiding the high overheads of larger agencies we’re able to offer you quality work at reasonable rates.

Centrally situated in the beautiful county of Suffolk, we welcome enquiries from an extended local client-base. We also have clients around the UK and internationally in Ireland, the US and Singapore.

Alex Adams: Founder / Director

Alex's LinkedIn profile

Alex has been designing, developing and managing software projects since 1998. His career has taken him around the UK and Ireland on projects as diverse as production line testing, digital audio, defence image exploitation, distributed computing systems, business systems, data marshalling systems, custom content management systems and interactive situational awareness.

He is a multidisciplinary developer and has worked with a number of languages, technologies and frameworks including: PHP, C#, C++, C, Java, JavaScript, Bash, MySQL, SQL Server, WordPress, ExpressionEngine, and Umbraco.

When he's not developing, he's a busy husband and dad who finds a bit of time to train for triathlon events, and he enjoys sailing, camping and caravanning around the UK and Europe with his family.

Sue Brettell: Creative Director

Sue's LinkedIn profile

Sue is a versatile designer and avid wordsmith on a mission to demystify website design for busy entrepreneurs. Her early work experience includes retail, recruitment, advertising, catering, property sales, illustration and, eventually her métier, publishing. Sue has been self-employed since 1989, increasing her repertoire from book design through branding to website design.

With a BA in 3D design from the Central School in London, Sue is also qualified as an executive secretary, hypnoanalyst, personal branding strategist and actor – a unique and unconventional combination that informs her work in UI design, copywriting and web presence optimisation.

Sue’s irrepressible enthusiasm for innovative design, new apps and advances in technology means she even spends her spare time researching and writing and has several blogs and ebooks in the pipeline.
